Privacy

Short version: I collect only what you type into the contact form, and nothing is sold or shared.

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Who I am

This is the personal portfolio of Rayner Dcunha, a student in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. It is not a business and it shows no ads. If you have a privacy question, use the contact form.

What I collect, and why

  • Contact form. The name, email address, subject and message you type. They are sent to a Google Apps Script that I run, which emails them to me through my Google account and sends you an automatic confirmation at the address you gave. I use them only to reply to you. Messages stay in my email inbox until I delete them.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted by Vercel and its domain name is managed by Cloudflare. Like any web host, they handle your IP address and basic request details to deliver pages and block abuse. I do not have a log of who visits.
  • Weather panel on the home page. Your browser guesses a city from your time zone (never from GPS or your exact location) and asks this site for the weather there. This site asks Open-Meteo on your behalf, so Open-Meteo never sees your IP address.
  • LeetCode count. The server fetches my own public LeetCode numbers. Nothing about you is sent.

What I do not do

  • No analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or cookies.
  • No fonts, scripts or images loaded from Google or other third-party sites. Fonts are served from this site.
  • I never sell or share your information.

Browser storage

The site uses your browser's session storage for three small flags: whether you have already seen the intro animation, a page-transition flag, and a 60-second pause between contact-form messages. They stay in your tab and disappear when you close it.

Other sites

Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, LeetCode, a published paper and other sites open those sites, which have their own policies. Links to other sites do not tell them which page you came from.

Your choices

You can ask me, through the contact form, to show you or delete a message you sent. Please do not put sensitive personal information in the form.

Changes

If this changes, I will update this page and the date above.
