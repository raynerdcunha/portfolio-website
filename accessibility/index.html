Accessibility

How this site works for people using keyboards and assistive technology, and what still needs work.

Last updated: November 24, 2026

My goal

I want this site to be usable by everyone, including people who use keyboards, screen readers or magnification. I aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. I have not had an outside audit.

What is in place

  • A "Skip to main content" link at the top of every page.
  • Pages use headings in order, landmarks (header, main, navigation, footer) and descriptive link text.
  • The site is built to be used with a keyboard, and interactive elements show a visible focus outline.
  • Images that carry meaning have text alternatives, and decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
  • Form fields have labels, and form errors and confirmations are announced to screen readers.
  • The skills graph on the Skills page has a plain list view.
  • On November 23, 2026, Google Lighthouse scored the home page 100 for accessibility on mobile. Automated tools only catch part of the problems, so this is not a guarantee.

Known limitations

  • Motion. The animated background and scroll effects currently run even if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Résumé PDF. It may not meet every accessibility criterion. If you need it in another format, ask me.

Tell me about a problem

If something on this site is hard to use, please tell me through the contact form, name the page, and describe what went wrong. I will do my best to fix it and to reply.
